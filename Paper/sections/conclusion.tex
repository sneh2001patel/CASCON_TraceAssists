\section{Conclusion}

LLMs struggle with information that is too large to read or too specific to
have appeared in their training data, and execution traces are both. We
presented \sysname, which answers plain-language questions about kernel
traces by having Java analyzers built on Trace Compass compute findings
from the whole trace, with RAG supplying documentation and an LLM
explaining the findings. On 13 questions over traces of 2, 10 and 30
minutes, it answered every question correctly, while the same model given
trace slices scored 35.5\% alone and 68.4\% with RAG, suggesting that
computed evidence, not retrieved text alone, is what makes such answers
reliable. Future work will remove the need for thread IDs in questions,
evaluate more programs and kinds of bugs, test \sysname without the
program documentation, and evaluate its statistical module.
